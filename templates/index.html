Prototype Data Sources

NE-SHIELD combines multiple geospatial datasets instead of relying on a single environmental variable.

Landslide Inventory

Geological Survey of India (GSI) landslide inventory used to construct the prototype training dataset.

Rainfall

NASA IMERG Final V07B climatology, June 2000–May 2023.

Terrain

NASA SRTMGL1 V003 elevation data. Terrain slope is derived from the DEM.

Soil

ISRIC SoilGrids clay, sand and silt composition for the surface soil layer.

AI Model

Algorithm: Random Forest Classifier

Features: annual rainfall, elevation, slope, clay, sand and silt.

Prototype Test Accuracy: 83.82%

ROC-AUC: 0.9059

The model estimates environmental landslide susceptibility.

Prototype Scope & Limitations

NE-SHIELD is currently demonstrated using Mizoram as the prototype study area.

Important:

The ML susceptibility score is not a guaranteed probability of a future landslide.

Recent rainfall thresholds are configurable prototype thresholds and are not official operational GSI thresholds.

Emergency response priority is a transparent rule-based decision-support score for the prototype.
