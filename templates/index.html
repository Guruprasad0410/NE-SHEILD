Reference-data foundation

Data & intelligence sources

The prototype draws on the environmental and landslide-inventory sources used to construct the reference dataset and train the current model.

Rainfall reference

NASA IMERG rainfall climatology provides the rainfall reference information used in the prototype dataset.

Source: NASA IMERG Final V07B

Terrain & elevation

Elevation and terrain-slope values support susceptibility modelling for the study area.

Source: NASA SRTMGL1 V003

Soil composition

Clay, sand and silt attributes are used as environmental model features.

Source: ISRIC SoilGrids

Landslide inventory

Historical inventory information supports construction and evaluation of the prototype training dataset.

Source: Geological Survey of India
Operational mandate & prototype scope

Decision support for vulnerable hillslopes

NE-SHIELD is a prototype that brings location-based susceptibility, rainfall-trigger, impact and priority calculations into one workflow. It is intended to support assessment and discussion; it is not an official warning system, live-monitoring service or guarantee of a future landslide.
